# Quantum-Inspired Gold Futures State-Space Model

An experimental quantitative research framework that applies quantum-mechanics-inspired mathematics to gold futures market dynamics.

The model maps price and volatility data into a one-dimensional wavefunction and evolves the state through stylized option-wall barriers using the time-dependent Schrödinger equation and split-step Fourier methods. Observed market prices are incorporated through Gaussian measurement updates, while the Wigner transform provides a phase-space representation of price and momentum dynamics.

The framework explores:

- Gold futures price and volatility state mapping
- Momentum-driven wave-packet evolution
- Call and put option walls as potential barriers
- Schrödinger equation numerical evolution
- Split-step Fourier / Strang-splitting methods
- Wigner phase-space analysis
- Wavefunction tunneling beyond market barriers
- Momentum, volatility, and wall-proximity diagnostics
- GLD options gamma mapping to GC when options data is available

The project combines quantitative finance, numerical methods, and concepts from quantum mechanics as an exploratory market-modeling framework.

This is not a quantum computer or a claim that financial markets are quantum systems. The quantum formalism is used as a mathematical modeling analogy. The framework is experimental and requires calibration, out-of-sample validation, transaction-cost analysis, and comparison with conventional quantitative models before any predictive interpretation.
